The Waccamaw People's Alliance is a member-founded body of the Waccamaw Indian
People, dedicated to keeping the tribe's record honest, its history preserved,
and its people informed.

## Our origin

The People's Alliance was founded in the early 1990s by **Iris Hatcher Ewing** —
Tribal Member #003 and the tribe's Second Chief, Internal. It began as a promise
to the membership: that someone would keep an honest accounting of the tribe's
affairs and answer to the people first. Iris built the tribe's earliest
membership records and reported back to members after every meeting. That work of
stewardship and transparency is the Alliance's founding purpose, and it continues
today.

## What we do

- **Preserve the record** — We maintain the tribe's digital archive and the
  [Waccamaw Museum](https://museum.waccamaw.org), keeping the history, governance,
  and milestones of the Waccamaw Indian People safe and sourced.
- **Keep the people informed** — We carry the tribe's public record, calendar, and
  communications to the membership through waccamaw.org.
- **Guard integrity** — We hold the tribe's public information to a standard of
  honesty and accuracy, in service of the members it belongs to.

## Stewardship

The People's Alliance is carried forward today by **Doug Hatcher** and
**Jennifer "Chell" Hatcher**, continuing the work Iris Ewing began.
