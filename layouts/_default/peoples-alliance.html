Our founder

Founded by Iris Hatcher Ewing

Iris founded the People’s Alliance in the early 1990s as a promise to the membership: that someone would keep an honest accounting of the tribe’s affairs and answer to the people first. She built the tribe’s earliest membership records and reported back to members after every meeting. That work of stewardship and transparency is the Alliance’s founding purpose — and it continues today.

To keep the tribe honest, its history preserved, and its people informed — answerable to the members first.
What we do

Preserve the record

We maintain the tribe’s digital archive and the Waccamaw Museum, keeping its history, governance, and milestones safe and sourced.

Keep the people informed

We carry the tribe’s public record, calendar, and communications to the membership through waccamaw.org.

Guard integrity

We hold the tribe’s public information to a standard of honesty and accuracy, in service of the members it belongs to.

Stewardship today

The People’s Alliance is carried forward today by Doug Hatcher and Jennifer “Chell” Hatcher, continuing the work Iris Ewing began.
